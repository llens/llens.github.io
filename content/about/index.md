I'm Thomas Snell, a researcher working at the intersection of quantum computing and evolutionary optimisation.

My current work focuses on using evolutionary algorithms, multi-objective search, and quality-diversity methods to automatically discover quantum circuits. I've built a fast numpy-based statevector simulator that enables large-scale benchmarking studies on commodity hardware.

## Research Interests

- **Quantum circuit synthesis** -- automatically discovering quantum algorithms via optimisation
- **Quality-diversity optimisation** -- MAP-Elites and other illumination algorithms for exploring the landscape of viable solutions
- **Multi-objective evolutionary algorithms** -- NSGA-II, Pareto-based search for fidelity-complexity trade-offs
- **Benchmarking and reproducibility** -- rigorous comparative studies across optimisation paradigms
