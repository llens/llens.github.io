I'm Thomas Snell, a Staff Software Engineer at [SoFi](https://www.sofi.com/) based in Helena, Montana. I have a passion for scientific computing and a knack for finding elegant, yet practical solutions to complex problems.

## Background

I hold a Bachelor of Physics from [Oxford University](https://www.ox.ac.uk/) and a PhD in Computational Geophysics from [Durham University](https://www.durham.ac.uk/), where I developed numerical simulations of fluid overpressure driven faulting and seismicity. My doctoral work delivered new efficient simulation techniques for non-smooth permeability evolution, with implications for earthquake forecasting and carbon capture and storage.

## Professional Experience

At SoFi, I've spent nearly seven years working across software engineering and fraud prevention. As Squad Lead for SAFE, SoFi's centralised fraud prevention tool, I built and scaled KYC and fraud decisioning systems handling thousands of daily applicants. Prior to SoFi, I co-founded [Aequicens](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tom-snell-59020540), where I delivered a market rating system combined with deep reinforcement learning portfolio management.

## Research Interests

- **Quantum circuit synthesis** -- automatically discovering quantum algorithms via optimisation
- **Quality-diversity optimisation** -- MAP-Elites and other illumination algorithms for exploring the landscape of viable solutions
- **Multi-objective evolutionary algorithms** -- NSGA-II, Pareto-based search for fidelity-complexity trade-offs
- **Computational geophysics** -- numerical simulation of subsurface fluid flow and fault mechanics
- **Benchmarking and reproducibility** -- rigorous comparative studies across optimisation paradigms
